## Levels of Prevention

### Primary Prevention
**Definition**: Preventing occurrence of STDs through health promotion and specific protection

#### ABC Approach (Adapted for India)
- **A - Abstinence**: Delay sexual debut; mutual fidelity in relationships
- **B - Be Faithful**: Mutual monogamy with tested partner
- **C - Condoms**: Consistent and correct use (male/female condoms)

#### Vaccination Programs
- **HPV Vaccination**: Cervarix (2-valent) or Gardasil (4/9-valent) for 9-26 years
- **Hepatitis B Vaccination**: Universal immunization; birth dose for infants
- **Coverage in India**: HBV (85%), HPV (limited state-wise implementation)

#### Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP)
- **Indication**: High-risk individuals (MSM, serodiscordant couples, IDUs)
- **Regimen**: Tenofovir/Emtricitabine daily or event-based
- **Availability**: Through NACO since 2017; expanding to more centers
- **Effectiveness**: 99% with adherence

#### Other Strategies
- **Blood Safety**: 100% voluntary blood donation; mandatory testing
- **Harm Reduction**: Needle exchange programs for IDUs
- **Safe Medical Practices**: Universal precautions in healthcare settings

### Secondary Prevention
**Definition**: Early detection and treatment to prevent complications and transmission

#### Screening Programs
- **ICTC Network**: 1,381 centers providing free HIV testing
- **Targeted Screening**: High-risk groups, antenatal women, TB patients
- **Provider-Initiated Testing**: Opt-out approach in clinical settings

#### Partner Notification Strategies
- **Voluntary Disclosure**: Patient informs partners
- **Provider-Assisted**: Healthcare worker contacts partners
- **Contract Referral**: Patient brings partner for testing
- **Effectiveness**: Increases case finding by 20-30%

#### Surveillance Systems
- **HIV Sentinel Surveillance**: Annual monitoring in high-risk areas
- **Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP)**: STD reporting
- **IBBS (Integrated Biological and Behavioural Surveillance)**: Risk behavior assessment

### Tertiary Prevention
**Definition**: Managing complications and preventing further deterioration

#### ART Centers and Linkages
- **1,200+ ART Centers**: Free treatment across India
- **CD4 Monitoring**: Every 6 months; viral load annually
- **Drug Resistance Testing**: When treatment failure suspected

#### Support Services
- **Community Care Centers (CCCs)**: Nutritional support, counseling
- **Positive People Networks**: Peer support groups
- **Rehabilitation Services**: For advanced disease complications

## National AIDS Control Programme (NACP)

### Programme Phases
- **Phase I (1992-1999)**: Pilot projects in 6 states
- **Phase II (1999-2006)**: Scaling up to all states
- **Phase III (2007-2012)**: 66% reduction in new infections
- **Phase IV (2012-2017)**: Sustained decline in HIV prevalence
- **Phase V (2017-2021)**: Test and treat strategy
- **Phase VI (2021-2026)**: Ending AIDS by 2030

### Key Achievements (NACO 2023)
- **New Infections**: Reduced by 66% (2007-2017)
- **ART Coverage**: 80% of PLHIV on treatment
- **Mortality**: 71% decline in AIDS-related deaths
- **Condom Distribution**: 800 million annually
- **ICTC Centers**: 1,381 established nationwide

### Current Strategies
- **Test and Treat**: Immediate ART initiation regardless of CD4 count
- **Prevention**: PrEP, PEP, condom promotion
- **Targeted Interventions**: For high-risk groups (MSM, FSWs, IDUs)
- **PPTCT**: Prevention of Parent-to-Child Transmission (95% coverage)

## Indian Context Challenges

### Socio-Cultural Barriers
- **Stigma and Discrimination**: Deep-rooted taboos around sexuality
- **Gender Inequalities**: Women have limited decision-making power
- **Religious and Caste Factors**: Influence healthcare-seeking behavior
- **Urbanization Impact**: Changing sexual behaviors increase risk

### Healthcare System Challenges
- **Access Disparities**: Rural-urban gaps in service delivery
- **Human Resources**: Shortage of trained counselors and providers
- **Supply Chain**: Ensuring consistent drug availability
- **Quality Assurance**: Maintaining standards across 1,200+ centers

### Epidemiological Challenges
- **Migration**: Interstate labor migration spreads infections
- **Condom Use**: Only 5.2% consistent use nationally
- **MSM and Transgender**: Legal discrimination affects service access
- **Co-infections**: TB-HIV common (10% of HIV patients)

### Economic and Policy Challenges
- **Funding Sustainability**: Donor-dependent programs
- **Integration**: With general health services
- **Monitoring**: Real-time data for decision-making
- **Innovation**: Adapting global strategies to local context

## Community Medicine Approaches

### Population-Level Interventions

#### Targeted Interventions (TI)
- **Core High-Risk Groups**: MSM, FSWs, IDUs, truckers, migrants
- **Components**: Outreach, condom distribution, STI treatment, behavior change
- **Coverage**: 1,017 TI projects across India
- **Impact**: Reduced HIV prevalence in targeted populations

#### Information, Education, Communication (IEC)
- **Mass Media Campaigns**: TV, radio, social media
- **School Programs**: Comprehensive sexuality education
- **Community Mobilization**: Local NGOs and peer educators
- **Workplace Interventions**: For industrial workers and uniformed services

#### Structural Interventions
- **Legal Reforms**: Anti-discrimination laws, transgender rights
- **Economic Empowerment**: Microfinance for FSWs
- **Healthcare Access**: Mobile clinics, telemedicine
- **Social Protection**: For vulnerable populations

### Surveillance and Monitoring

#### Case-Based Surveillance
- **HIV Case Reporting**: Through HIV Case Reporting System
- **STI Surveillance**: Sentinel sites in STI clinics
- **Death Reporting**: AIDS-related mortality tracking

#### Program Monitoring
- **Performance Indicators**: ART coverage, viral suppression rates
- **Quality Assurance**: External evaluations and audits
- **Research**: Operational research for program improvement

## Prevention Strategies by STD Type

### Bacterial STDs
- **Condom Use**: 50-80% reduction in gonorrhea/chlamydia
- **Partner Treatment**: Essential for cure and prevention
- **Antimicrobial Stewardship**: Preventing resistance
- **Screening**: High-risk groups and symptomatic individuals

### Viral STDs
- **Vaccination**: HBV and HPV primary prevention
- **Harm Reduction**: For blood-borne transmission
- **Early Treatment**: ART for HIV, antivirals for HSV
- **Suppressive Therapy**: Reduces transmission (HSV, HIV)

### General Strategies
- **ABC Approach**: Comprehensive risk reduction
- **Condom Programming**: Social marketing and distribution
- **IEC Campaigns**: Addressing myths and stigma
- **Integration**: HIV-STD-RTI services

- **STI Incidence**: Reported cases and trends
- **Condom Use**: Consistent use rates
- **Knowledge Levels**: STD awareness in communities

### Impact Indicators
- **New Infections**: Reduction in HIV/STI incidence
- **Mortality**: AIDS-related deaths
- **Quality of Life**: For PLHIV
- **Stigma Reduction**: Community attitudes

## Future Directions

### Innovation in Prevention
- **Digital Health**: Apps for PrEP adherence, telemedicine
- **Self-Testing**: HIV self-test kits for wider access
- **Long-Acting Prevention**: Injectable PrEP, vaginal rings
- **Integration**: With maternal health, TB, and NCD programs

### Research Priorities
- **Vaccine Development**: HIV vaccine trials
- **Cure Research**: Functional cure for HIV
- **Implementation Science**: Scaling up effective interventions
- **Health Systems Strengthening**: Universal health coverage

## Key Facts for MBBS Students
- Prevention is more cost-effective than treatment
- Community-based approaches are essential in India
- Stigma is the biggest barrier to effective prevention
- Integration of services improves outcomes
- Monitoring and evaluation drive program improvement
