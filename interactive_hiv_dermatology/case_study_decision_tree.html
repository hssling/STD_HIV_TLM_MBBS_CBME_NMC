Case Study: 28-year-old Male with Skin Lesions

Patient History: A 28-year-old male presents with multiple purple-red patches on his legs and oral mucosa. He reports weight loss, night sweats, and fatigue. CD4 count: 180 cells/μL. Lives in Mumbai, India.

Clinical Findings: Multiple violaceous plaques on lower extremities and hard palate. No fever currently.

Decision Tree: Diagnostic Approach

Kaposi's Sarcoma
Bacillary Angiomatosis

What would you do next for suspected Kaposi's Sarcoma?
Biopsy for histopathology
Check CD4 count only
Start ART immediately

Correct!

Biopsy shows spindle cells, vascular slits, and HHV-8 positivity. This confirms Kaposi's Sarcoma.

Indian Context: Kaposi's Sarcoma is seen in 10-15% of Indian HIV patients, often oral involvement.

Incorrect

Correct!

No drug history reported. The lesions are more consistent with Kaposi's Sarcoma than drug rash.

Incorrect

Abacavir hypersensitivity is usually earlier and more widespread, not localized purple patches.

Incorrect

Nevirapine rash is usually morbilliform, not violaceous plaques.

Patient is 28, lesions are recent. Vascular malformations are usually congenital.

Incorrect
