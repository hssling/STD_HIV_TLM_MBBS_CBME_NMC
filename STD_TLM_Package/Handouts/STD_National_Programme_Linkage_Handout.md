## National AIDS Control Programme (NACP VI: 2021-2026)

### Vision and Goals
- **Vision**: "Ending AIDS by 2030" (SDG 3.3)
- **Goals**:
  - Reduce new HIV infections by 80% by 2025
  - Reduce AIDS-related deaths by 80% by 2025
  - Eliminate vertical transmission by 2025
  - Eliminate stigma and discrimination

### Key Strategies
1. **Test and Treat**: Immediate ART initiation
2. **Prevention**: PrEP, PEP, condom promotion
3. **Targeted Interventions**: For high-risk groups
4. **Integration**: With general health services

### Achievements (2023)
- **ART Coverage**: 80% of PLHIV (1.6 million on treatment)
- **Viral Suppression**: 90% among adherent patients
- **New Infections**: Reduced by 66% since 2007
- **Infrastructure**: 1,381 ICTCs, 1,200+ ART centers

## Integration with Revised National TB Control Programme (RNTCP)

### Rationale for Integration
- **TB-HIV Co-infection**: 10% of HIV patients have TB
- **HIV Impact on TB**: Increases TB incidence 20-30 fold
- **Shared Risk Factors**: Poverty, migration, malnutrition
- **Program Efficiency**: Combined service delivery reduces costs

### Collaborative Activities
- **HIV Testing in TB Patients**: Provider-initiated testing and counseling (PITC)
- **TB Screening in PLHIV**: Intensified TB case finding
- **Co-treatment**: ART and ATT management
- **Infection Control**: In healthcare settings

### Operational Linkages
- **Joint Training**: Staff trained in both programs
- **Shared Infrastructure**: Combined clinics and laboratories
- **Data Integration**: Unified reporting systems
- **Community Outreach**: Joint IEC campaigns

### Success Metrics
- **TB-HIV Case Finding**: 80% of TB patients tested for HIV
- **ART Coverage in TB-HIV**: 90% initiated on ART
- **TB Treatment Success**: Maintained high rates despite HIV
- **Mortality Reduction**: 50% reduction in TB-HIV deaths

## Integration with National Viral Hepatitis Control Programme (NVHCP)

### Programme Components
- **Hepatitis B Vaccination**: Universal immunization since 2002
- **Blood Safety**: 100% screening of blood donations
- **Harm Reduction**: For IDUs and high-risk groups
- **Treatment Access**: Free antivirals through government facilities

### HIV-HBV Co-infection Management
- **Prevalence**: 10-15% of PLHIV have chronic HBV
- **Treatment**: Tenofovir covers both HIV and HBV
- **Monitoring**: Combined viral load testing
- **Prevention**: Birth dose vaccination for infants

### Operational Integration
- **Testing**: Combined HIV-HBV screening in high-risk groups
- **Treatment**: Unified ART regimens
- **Surveillance**: Integrated reporting
- **IEC**: Joint awareness campaigns

---

## Integration with Reproductive and Child Health Programme (RCH)

### Prevention of Parent-to-Child Transmission (PPTCT)
- **Coverage**: 95% of pregnant women tested
- **Strategy**: Opt-out testing in antenatal clinics
- **Treatment**: ART for HIV-positive pregnant women
- **Infant Testing**: DNA PCR at 6 weeks, 6 months, 18 months

### Components
- **Antenatal Care**: Integrated HIV testing
- **Delivery**: Safe delivery practices, AZT prophylaxis
- **Postnatal**: Infant feeding counseling, follow-up
- **Family Planning**: Dual protection counseling

### Achievements
- **Vertical Transmission**: Reduced to <2% with ART
- **Infant Testing**: 95% coverage
- **ART Coverage**: 90% of HIV-positive pregnant women
- **Institutional Deliveries**: Increased through program linkages

## Integration with National Programme for Prevention and Control of Cancer (NPCC)

### Cervical Cancer and HPV
- **Burden**: 2nd most common cancer in Indian women
- **HPV Prevalence**: 20-25 million infected women
- **Screening**: VIA/VILI in rural areas
- **Vaccination**: HPV vaccine introduction in select states

### Operational Linkages
- **Screening Camps**: Combined with HIV testing
- **Treatment**: Integrated ART for HIV-positive cancer patients
- **Surveillance**: Cancer registry integration
- **IEC**: Joint awareness on HPV and HIV

## Integration with National Health Mission (NHM)

### Ayushman Bharat and HIV Services
- **Primary Healthcare**: HIV testing at Health and Wellness Centers
- **AB-PMJAY**: Free treatment packages for HIV/AIDS
- **Community Health Officers**: Trained in HIV management
- **Telemedicine**: Remote consultation for ART follow-up

### Convergence Strategies
- **Infrastructure**: Utilizing HWCs for HIV services
- **Human Resources**: Multi-skilling of healthcare workers
- **Supply Chain**: Integrated drug procurement
- **Monitoring**: Unified health information systems

## State-Level Program Variations

### High-Prevalence States
- **Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana**
- **Strategies**: Intensive TI programs, large ART networks
- **Achievements**: Significant HIV prevalence decline
- **Challenges**: Migration, urban slums

### Northeast Region
- **Nagaland (1.5%), Manipur (1.4%)**
- **Approaches**: Community-based interventions
- **Success Factors**: Strong civil society involvement
- **Challenges**: Conflict areas, cross-border issues

### Southern States
- **Tamil Nadu, Kerala**
- **Models**: Public-private partnerships
- **Innovations**: Self-help groups, microfinance
- **Outcomes**: Low HIV prevalence, high ART coverage

## Community Medicine Role in Program Implementation

### Surveillance and Monitoring
- **HIV Sentinel Surveillance**: Annual surveys in high-risk areas
- **Integrated Disease Surveillance**: STD outbreak monitoring
- **Program Evaluation**: Coverage, quality, impact assessment
- **Research**: Operational research for program improvement

### Capacity Building
- **Training Programs**: For healthcare providers and counselors
- **IEC Campaigns**: Community awareness and behavior change
- **Peer Education**: Community health workers and PLHIV networks
- **Quality Assurance**: Regular audits and supportive supervision

### Advocacy and Policy
- **Stigma Reduction**: Community mobilization campaigns
- **Rights-Based Approach**: Ensuring access for vulnerable groups
- **Resource Mobilization**: Partnerships with NGOs and donors
- **Policy Integration**: Mainstreaming HIV in general health services

---

## Challenges in Program Integration

### Operational Challenges
- **Human Resources**: Shortage of trained personnel
- **Infrastructure**: Limited facilities in rural areas
- **Supply Chain**: Drug stockouts and quality issues
- **Data Systems**: Integration of multiple reporting platforms

### Socio-Political Challenges
- **Stigma**: Prevents people from accessing services
- **Migration**: Affects continuity of care
- **Funding**: Donor dependency and sustainability
- **Political Commitment**: Varies across states

### Technical Challenges
- **Drug Resistance**: Emerging resistance patterns
- **Co-infections**: Complex management protocols
- **Monitoring**: Real-time data for decision-making
- **Innovation**: Adapting global strategies to local context
