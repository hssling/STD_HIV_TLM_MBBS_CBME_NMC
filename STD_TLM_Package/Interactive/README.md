# STD Interactive Materials

This collection of interactive HTML/JavaScript components is designed for MBBS 3rd year students to learn about Sexually Transmitted Diseases (STDs) from a community medicine perspective, with focus on Indian context.

## Components

### 1. Clickable Diagram - STD Transmission Routes and Clinical Features
- **File**: `clickable_diagram.html`
- **Description**: Interactive diagram showing STD transmission routes and common clinical features
- **Features**: Click on transmission routes (sexual, vertical, blood, sharing needles) and clinical features (syphilis, gonorrhea, chlamydia, HIV, hepatitis, herpes)
- **Educational Focus**: Visual understanding of transmission dynamics and clinical presentations with Indian epidemiology

### 2. Case Study with Decision Tree
- **File**: `case_study_decision_tree.html`
- **Description**: Interactive case study of a 25-year-old female with vaginal discharge and pelvic pain
- **Features**: Step-by-step diagnostic decision tree with multiple choice questions for gonorrhea/chlamydia diagnosis
- **Educational Focus**: Clinical decision-making process, differential diagnosis, Indian treatment protocols

### 3. Symptom Checker
- **File**: `symptom_checker.html`
- **Description**: Tool to input patient symptoms and get likely STD diagnoses with probability scoring
- **Features**: Multi-select symptoms, likelihood assessment, contextual recommendations
- **Educational Focus**: Pattern recognition, differential diagnosis based on symptoms, Indian context considerations

### 4. Treatment Simulator
- **File**: `treatment_simulator.html`
- **Description**: Simulate treatment outcomes for gonorrhea with PID
- **Features**: Choose treatment regimens, see realistic outcomes and progress tracking
- **Educational Focus**: Treatment decision-making, understanding antibiotic resistance, Indian treatment guidelines

### 5. Interactive Quiz
- **File**: `interactive_quiz.html`
- **Description**: 10-question multiple choice quiz on STD epidemiology and management
- **Features**: Immediate feedback, explanations, progress tracking, restart option
- **Educational Focus**: Knowledge assessment, reinforcement of key concepts, Indian public health perspective

### 6. Gamified Prevention Strategy Game
- **File**: `gamified_prevention_game.html`
- **Description**: Interactive game building a prevention pyramid with multi-level strategies
- **Features**: Four-level pyramid (individual, community, health system, policy), scoring system, progressive difficulty
- **Educational Focus**: Comprehensive prevention approaches, NACO strategies, Indian public health framework

### 7. Virtual Patient Simulation with Branching Scenarios
- **File**: `virtual_patient_branching.html`
- **Description**: Complex patient scenarios with branching decision trees and scoring
- **Features**: Four realistic cases, evidence-based choices, immediate feedback, progress tracking
- **Educational Focus**: Clinical decision-making, NACO treatment protocols, Indian context considerations

### 8. Role-Playing Counseling Scenario
- **File**: `role_playing_counseling.html`
- **Description**: Interactive counseling practice with ethical dilemmas and cultural sensitivity
- **Features**: Two counseling scenarios, empathy scoring, feedback on responses, learning points
- **Educational Focus**: Communication skills, cultural competence, ethical counseling in Indian context

### 9. Interactive Multimedia Timeline of STD Programs
- **File**: `multimedia_timeline.html`
- **Description**: Historical timeline of STD control programs in India with multimedia elements
- **Features**: Decade-wise navigation, detailed program information, statistics, visual elements
- **Educational Focus**: Evolution of STD programs, NACO milestones, policy development in India

## Instructions for Use

### For Students
1. Open any HTML file in a modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
2. Follow the on-screen instructions for each interactive component
3. Complete all components for comprehensive learning experience
4. Use the quiz to test your knowledge

### For Instructors
1. These materials can be used in classroom settings or online learning platforms
2. Components can be embedded in presentations using iframe tags
3. Use the case study and treatment simulator for group discussions
4. Quiz results can be used for formative assessment

### Integration into Presentations
```html
<iframe src="path/to/interactive_component.html" width="800" height="600"></iframe>
```

## Educational Objectives

By the end of these interactive materials, students should be able to:

1. Understand STD transmission routes and risk factors in Indian context
2. Recognize clinical features and complications of common STDs
3. Apply systematic approach to STD diagnosis and management
4. Understand national STD control programs and treatment protocols
5. Appreciate the public health impact of STDs in India

## Content Validation

All medical content has been developed based on:
- WHO guidelines for STD management
- Indian National AIDS Control Organisation (NACO) protocols
- National STD Control Programme guidelines
- Current epidemiological data from Indian studies
- Clinical experience in Indian healthcare settings

## Key Indian Context Highlights

- **Epidemiology**: HIV prevalence 0.22%, syphilis 1-2% in high-risk groups
- **Transmission**: Heterosexual contact predominant (86% of HIV cases)
- **Challenges**: Antibiotic resistance, under-diagnosis, stigma
- **Programs**: NACO, NVBDCP integration, free treatment availability
- **Cultural factors**: Stigma, delayed diagnosis, traditional remedies
